\section*{句型}

\begin{enumerate}

\item \textbf{引言/谚语类}
\begin{verbatim}
As the proverb says, ...
It's universally acknowledged that ______.
As is ______, ...
You're the architect of your own future.
\end{verbatim}

\item \textbf{对比/转折类}
\begin{verbatim}
On the contrary, ...
\end{verbatim}

\item \textbf{虚拟/建议类}
\begin{verbatim}
It's high time that we + did ______.
I think it reasonable ______.
Only in this way can you ______.
Under no circumstance could I ______ without your assistance.
\end{verbatim}

\item \textbf{总结/结论类}
\begin{verbatim}
Taking all these factors into consideration, 
we naturally come to the conclusion that ______.
\end{verbatim}

\item \textbf{建议/请求类}
\begin{verbatim}
We should ______.
Please have no hesitation in ______.
Thus, it is imperative to ______.
\end{verbatim}

\item \textbf{评价类}
\begin{verbatim}
The ______ itself was undoubtedly wonderful, 
but I have ample faith that it would be better if ______.
\end{verbatim}

\item \textbf{情绪/感受类}
\begin{verbatim}
a rollercoaster ride of emotions.  （情绪起伏很大的时期）
magnify the beauty in our life.   （放大生活中的美好）
\end{verbatim}

\end{enumerate}

\section*{短语与句子集锦}

\begin{enumerate}

\item \textbf{活动体验类}
\begin{verbatim}
Challenging as the activity was, everyone enjoyed it 
and all the participants benefited a lot.
\end{verbatim}

\item \textbf{功能/角色类}
\begin{verbatim}
... where the role that A plays can ... in.
\end{verbatim}

\item \textbf{对比类}
\begin{verbatim}
There is no straightforward equivalence between A and B.
（A与B之间绝非轻易等同。）
\end{verbatim}

\item \textbf{基础/铺垫类}
\begin{verbatim}
lay the foundation (to) sth / pave the way for
\end{verbatim}

\item \textbf{核心论点}
\begin{verbatim}
The central argument is that capitalism sows the seeds 
of its own destruction.
\end{verbatim}

\item \textbf{持久性}
\begin{verbatim}
Withstand the test of time. （经得起时间考验）
\end{verbatim}

\item \textbf{益处表达}
\begin{verbatim}
... possesses a lot of benefits.
\end{verbatim}

\item \textbf{双重益处（倒装结构）}
\begin{verbatim}
Not only will it afford young people the opportunity to master ..., 
but also increase the likelihood of a more promising future for them.
\end{verbatim}

\item \textbf{跨文化/视野类}
\begin{verbatim}
... person with cosmopolitan outlook through bonding 
with peers from diverse backgrounds.
\end{verbatim}

\item \textbf{多样性意识}
\begin{verbatim}
Gaining the consciousness that proximity to diversity attracts ...
is another justification for ...
\end{verbatim}

\item \textbf{结交朋友/视野}
\begin{verbatim}
Making acquaintance of people with diverse cultures 
gives you a broader world view, and sophistication.
\end{verbatim}

\item \textbf{情绪/活力类}
\begin{verbatim}
Suppress my exhilaration to ... grasp the precious 
inject vitality into my work.
\end{verbatim}

\item \textbf{逃离日常}
\begin{verbatim}
It also provides a chance to escape from the dull routine 
and get our exhausted mind refreshed.
\end{verbatim}

\end{enumerate}
