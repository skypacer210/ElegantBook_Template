\section*{\textit{Our World}}

If we could build a world with our bare hands, it would not be made of steel or concrete, but of dreams.

\vspace{0.5cm}

In my corner stands \textbf{Zero Stress Land}. There is a roller coaster that moves so slowly you can drink tea on it, a Nap Carousel of giant fluffy beds, and the Ice Cream Lab, serving unlimited flavors—even tofu and chili, because strangeness is celebrated here.

\vspace{0.5cm}

But the soul of this world is your \textbf{Fairy Tale Garden}. At night, blue, silver, and white flowers glow softly, lighting up tiny whimsical houses. It is a place where you feel relaxed, magical, and at peace—like you belong in a dream.

\vspace{0.5cm}

To visit the garden, you must first ride the \textbf{Atlantis Wing}. It flies at lightning speed, diving into the lost city. Nearby, the \textbf{Loop Tower} offers a thrilling drop for those who crave a rush.

\vspace{0.5cm}

Yet the deepest journey is the \textbf{Atlantis Dark Ride}. You step into a small boat that drifts into the sleeping city. You pass a beautiful palace—its walls old and cracked, yet quietly beautiful, unlike the grand palaces of Europe. Then, your boat slips into the sea. Beneath the waves, the stars have not disappeared; they sparkle all around you. The magic of Atlantis lives here, in the silence. The current carries you back to the palace door, and you return to the surface, changed and comforted.

\vspace{0.5cm}

In this world, work exists for only two days a week. The rest is for resting under trees, reading in floating hammocks, or walking through gardens that light themselves.

\vspace{0.5cm}

There are no traffic jams, only glowing rivers and paths of flowers. It is a world where magic is real, peace is endless, and everyone is allowed to simply… breathe.

\vspace{0.5cm}

This is our world. Welcome home.

\section*{\textit{Our World-v2}}


Were we to construct a world with our bare hands, it would be shaped not from steel or concrete, but woven entirely from dreams.

In my corner of this realm lies \textbf{Zero Stress Land}. Here, a roller coaster crawls at such a leisurely pace that one could comfortably sip tea while riding it; a Nap Carousel features enormous, cloud-like beds, and the Ice Cream Lab serves up an endless array of unconventional flavours—including tofu and chilli—for eccentricity is wholeheartedly embraced here.

Nevertheless, the very heart and soul of this world is your \textbf{Fairy Tale Garden}. As dusk falls, blossoms in shades of blue, silver, and white emit a gentle glow, illuminating a cluster of quaint, whimsical cottages. This sanctuary offers an overwhelming sense of relaxation and wonder, where one feels a profound sense of belonging, as if stepping directly into a living dream.

However, entry to this enchanted garden is conditional upon experiencing the \textbf{Atlantis Wing} first. This extraordinary ride soars at tremendous speed before plunging into the depths of the sunken metropolis. Adjacent to it, the \textbf{Loop Tower} offers an exhilarating free-fall for those who crave an adrenaline rush.

Yet, the most profound voyage is unquestionably the \textbf{Atlantis Dark Ride}. You board a tiny vessel that gently drifts into the heart of the sleeping city. Gliding past a magnificent palace, you notice its walls—though aged and weathered—possess a subtle, understated beauty, standing in stark contrast to the grandeur of European castles. Suddenly, your boat emerges into the open sea. Beneath the surface, the stars have not vanished; instead, they glisten and dance all around you. The enchantment of Atlantis lingers in this serene silence. Ultimately, the gentle current guides you back to the palace entrance, and as you resurface, you emerge feeling profoundly moved and deeply soothed.

In this utopia, the working week amounts to a mere two days. The remainder of one's time is dedicated to tranquillity—whether dozing beneath leafy canopies, immersing oneself in a book while suspended in a floating hammock, or meandering through gardens that illuminate themselves at twilight.

Traffic congestion is non-existent here, replaced by luminous rivers and winding flower-laden pathways. It is a realm where the impossible becomes tangible, where peace is boundless, and where each individual is granted the liberty to simply… pause and catch their breath.

\vspace{0.5cm}

    \textbf{This is our vision. Welcome home.}
